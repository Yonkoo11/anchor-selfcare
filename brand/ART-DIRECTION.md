# ART-DIRECTION — ANCHOR

## The lazy idea, named and forbidden

**The nautical anchor.** For a product called ANCHOR it is the name-literal, the first thing
anyone draws, and it imports sailing, boats and navy baggage into a product about being too
tired to shower. It also collides with every "anchor" brand already on the internet: zero
ownability. Forbidden.

## Signature element

**Drift coming to rest.** One continuous stroke that begins as a wavering line and resolves
into stillness at a single solid point. Input (drift) → process (the settling curve) →
resolved output (rest), as one object, not three icons in a tray.

This encodes the core verb literally: the mark *is* the act of steadying. It also encodes
sufficiency, because what stops the drift is one small point, not a large apparatus.

## Material language

Flat, matte, warm. Generous stroke weight with round caps, the way a soft pencil rests.
No gloss, no glass, no depth, no shadow. The product's whole design system is "natural
materials, aged paper, nothing shiny" and the mark must speak that language.

## Composition

Cream mark on a solid sage-to-moss field, full bleed. Composed inside the **circle** X will
crop to, with the mark's extremes kept well inside the inscribed circle. Optically centered,
generous breathing room, one object only.

## Palette

Field: sage `#7D8B7A` → moss `#6B7B6A`. Mark: cream `#FAF8F5`. Nothing else.

## Forbidden list (gate at entry, auto-reject at judging)

Universal: no text, letters or numbers in the mark. No trademarks or OS glyphs. No rainbow or
second hue. No sparkles, glow, lens flare or gradient mesh. No busy composition. No stacked
motifs. No 3D bevel or drop shadow.

Product-specific clichés, all forbidden:

| Because ANCHOR is… | Cliché to forbid |
|---|---|
| named Anchor | the nautical anchor, rope, chain, ship's wheel |
| a wellness app | lotus, leaf, heart, meditating figure, sunrise, mandala, water droplet |
| adjacent to mental health | brain, head silhouette, medical cross, pill |
| calm/breathing adjacent | concentric "breathing" rings as the whole idea, ripple wallpaper |

## Dual legibility target

At **16px** the silhouette must still read as "a line that settles onto a point."
At **512px** the easing of the settling curve is the craft worth looking at.
