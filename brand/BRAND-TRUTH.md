# BRAND-TRUTH — ANCHOR

Extracted from the codebase, not invented: DESIGN_SYSTEM.md (palette), app/layout.tsx
(typography and metadata), public/ (the icon set being superseded), README.md (voice).

- **name**: ANCHOR
- **purpose**: A free self-care guide that asks for the smallest possible thing when you are
  too overwhelmed to do anything else.
- **core_verb**: steady. Not "track", not "improve", not "optimize". To hold still something
  that is drifting.
- **metaphor**: the smallest weight that stops the drift. The moment wavering becomes rest.
  NOT a ship's anchor. The product's own line is "one shower is enough" — the mark should
  encode sufficiency, not effort.
- **palette**: one accent, sage `#7D8B7A` into moss `#6B7B6A`. Neutrals cream `#FAF8F5`,
  bark `#2D2A26`. No other hue enters the mark.
- **constraints**:
  - Size floor **48px** (X timeline avatar) and **16px** (favicon). Both are hard.
  - X crops avatars to a **circle** — the mark must be composed for a circle, not a square.
  - Flat vector. The product is flat, warm and matte; a glossy 3D icon would lie about it.
  - Supersedes the current icon set, which reads **"SC"** — wrong initials, left over from an
    earlier name. Every icon in public/ is currently wrong.
